    Play Anywhere

    Keep Sakura Springs one tap away on your phone, tablet or computer.

    Computer (Chrome / Edge)
    1. Open the full-screen app page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
    2. Chrome: click the install icon in the address bar, or ⋮ → Cast, save, and share → Install page as app…. Edge: … → Apps → Install this site as an app.
    3. Click Install.
    4. Sakura Springs now opens in its own window from your desktop, taskbar or Start menu.
    iPhone & iPad
    1. Open the full-screen app page in Safari.
    2. Tap the Share button: the square with an arrow pointing up.
    3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
    4. Tap Add. Sakura Springs opens full screen from its new icon.
    Android
    1. Open the full-screen app page in Chrome.
    2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
    3. Tap Install app. On some phones it says Add to Home screen.
    4. Tap Install, then open Sakura Springs from your home screen.

    Good to know: this adds a web app, not an app-store download. It runs in your browser's engine, works offline after the first visit, and saves your garden on that device. Progress in the app is separate from progress on itch.io.
