Akari Atlas: Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: November 7, 2026

What the app stores

Your trip details (dates, places, budget, notes, checklists) are stored on your device. If you tap "Save to my Google account", a copy is saved in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (scope drive.appdata). The app can only see that one file. It cannot see any other files in your Drive. The developer never receives or sees this data.

If you use the optional shared-sync invite link instead, your trip is stored in a private Google Sheet run by the app's developer and is readable only with your trip code and key.

Location

Your location is used only on your device, to show nearby places and give walking directions. It is not stored or sent anywhere, except as coordinates in requests to the map, weather and routing services listed below.

Third-party services

The app shows data from OpenStreetMap (Photon, Overpass, OSRM routing), Open-Meteo weather, open.er-api.com exchange rates, Google Maps embeds and Taiwan MOTC TDX. Their own privacy policies apply.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's website data on your phone. To remove the Drive copy, go to Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → "Akari Atlas" → Delete hidden app data, then remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Terms

The app is free and provided as-is, without warranty. Travel information such as prices, schedules and opening hours may be out of date, so always confirm with official sources.
