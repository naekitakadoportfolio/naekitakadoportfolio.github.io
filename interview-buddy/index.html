How it works

  1. Resumes: upload your CV. Check what the AI reads.
  2. Job posts: paste the job post and your notes.
  3. Settings: add your Claude key (and Deepgram for live interviews).
  4. Mock interview: practice out loud and get a score.
  5. Live interview: open your meeting tab, click Start, pick the tab and turn on Share tab audio. Answers appear on their own.
  6. Stories: save 4–6 real stories so answers use true examples.
  7. Prep: one click builds likely questions, weak spots, questions to ask and your rate script.
  8. 🪟 Float over Meet: during a live interview, pop the answer into a small always-on-top window under your webcam.
  9. Maker: tailor your resume to each job, edit it, check the ATS keyword match, write a cover letter, download unlimited PDFs.
  10. Headshot: upload or take a photo, replace the background, adjust light, or generate an AI studio headshot — then put it on your resume.
  11. Code: practice coding problems — snap a screenshot, get hints in steps, review your code, explain it out loud.
  12. Tracker: stages, follow-up dates and ready-to-send emails for every job.
  13. Demo: add #demo to the link to show the app with sample data.
  14. History: every live interview, debrief, mock and prep pack is saved here.
  15. Profiles (top right): keep each job separate. Settings → Back up moves everything to another computer.
