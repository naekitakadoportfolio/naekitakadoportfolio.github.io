Independent Product BuildLive · actively developed

Akari Atlas

A travel-planning and travel-management app that brings together weather, place discovery, maps, day-by-day itineraries, trip budgeting, currency conversion, travel documents and checklists.

Key features

  • Itinerary planning with an exportable PDF
  • Place search and map integration
  • Weather and currency information
  • Budget and spending tracking
  • Travel documents and checklists
  • Installable, responsive app

My contribution

Designed, built and maintain the whole product: interface, offline-capable app shell, data model, integrations and PDF export.

Technologies

JavaScript PWA (service worker) · Leaflet + OpenStreetMap (Photon, Nominatim) · Open-Meteo · open.er-api.com · jsPDF · Google Drive app-data backup · Apps Script backend for optional shared sync · GitHub Pages + GitHub Actions checks

Privacy & functionality notes

Trip data is stored on the device; a Google Drive copy and shared sync are optional. Weather, exchange rates, transit and opening hours come from third-party services and may be delayed or inaccurate, so confirm important details with official sources. Some local-transit features currently cover Taiwan only. The privacy policy lists the optional Gmail import and translation service.

Meadow Money

A personal finance and savings tracker for organizing income, expenses, receipts, budgets, recurring bills and savings goals.

Key features

  • Income and expense tracking
  • Receipt capture
  • Budgets and recurring bills
  • Savings jars and goals
  • Insights and CSV export
  • Optional Google Drive backup

My contribution

Designed, built and maintain the product, including the on-device mode, Google Drive storage model and Android packaging.

Technologies

Single-page JavaScript PWA · on-device storage · Google Identity Services · Google Sheets and Drive APIs (drive.file scope only) · Google Picker · GitHub Pages + GitHub Actions · Android app package

Privacy & functionality notes

With 'Start now', records are saved on your device only. If you choose Google Drive backup, they are kept in a spreadsheet and folder in your own Drive that the app creates; the app can't see your other files. No developer server or database. This is a personal budgeting tool, not a bank, payment service or financial adviser.

Koi & Cosmos

An experimental productivity and wellness app combining shift tracking, tasks, stand-up movement reminders, music, sleep and wake routines, and personalized themes.

Key features

  • Shift tracking
  • Task management
  • Stand-up and movement reminders
  • Spotify music integration
  • Sleep and wake routines
  • Personalized visual themes

My contribution

Concept, interaction design and front-end build, including the animated koi timer and music integration.

Technologies

JavaScript PWA (service worker) · Spotify Web API and Web Playback SDK · Open-Meteo geocoding · Screen Wake Lock API · on-device storage · GitHub Pages

Privacy & functionality notes

Data stays on the device (see the in-app privacy policy). Reminders and alarms only fire while the app is open; browsers don't reliably run background alarms or play audio in the background, so keep the screen on for the wake alarm. Spotify playback needs a Spotify Premium account, and while the Spotify app is in development mode only approved test users can connect.

Questions

Frequently asked

What hours do you work?

I'm based in the Philippines (UTC+8) and regularly work US business hours. We agree on fixed overlap hours at the start.

Which tools do you work with?

Mainly Google Workspace and Apps Script, ClickUp, Zapier, HubSpot, Slack, QuickBooks and Jira, plus REST APIs, webhooks and Playwright. If a tool is new to me, I'll say so up front.

How does billing work?

Audits and builds are quoted as fixed-scope projects after a call. Ongoing support is a monthly retainer with agreed hours.

How do you handle confidential information?

I work inside your accounts with the access you grant, keep credentials in your password manager, and never reuse client data. Everything on this site uses sample or anonymized data.

Will automations send anything without approval?

No. Anything that communicates with clients or makes decisions includes a human approval step unless you decide otherwise.
